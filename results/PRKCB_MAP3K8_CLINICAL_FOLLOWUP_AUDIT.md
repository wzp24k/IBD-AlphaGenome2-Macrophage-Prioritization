# PRKCB and MAP3K8 clinical-translation follow-up

## Sotrastaurin UC trial

- Trial: NCT00572585 / CAEB071A2210.
- Phase: 2b, randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled.
- Dose: 300 mg twice daily for 2 weeks, then 200 mg twice daily for 2 weeks.
- Population: 42 sotrastaurin and 18 placebo participants.
- Clinical remission: 6/36 (16.7%) with sotrastaurin versus 0/17 (0%) with placebo.
- Posterior difference: 12.2% (95% credibility interval -2.1% to 27.4%).
- Probability of any improvement: 95.5%.
- Probability of a difference of at least 20%: 14.2%.
- Partial Mayo change at day 29: -3.7 versus -1.9.
- Any adverse event: 73.8% versus 55.6%.
- Gastrointestinal adverse events: 61.9% versus 22.2%.
- Renal and urinary adverse events: 28.6% versus 0%.
- Serious adverse events: 7.1% versus 11.1%.
- The study was terminated early for strategic reasons. No primary peer-reviewed publication was listed.

The efficacy interval crosses zero, the confirmatory evidence is absent and the
trial was small and early-terminated. A treatment recommendation is not supported.

## Drug selectivity

- Sotrastaurin is annotated as a pan-PKC inhibitor covering 11 PKC-family targets.
- Enzastaurin and ruboxistaurin are annotated as PRKCB-selective inhibitors, but no IBD trial was identified for either drug.
- Enzastaurin and other PRKCB drugs were developed primarily in oncology or non-IBD indications.

## Risk direction

- PRKCB: the risk allele increases PRKCB expression; inhibitor action is directionally compatible.
- MAP3K8: the risk allele decreases MAP3K8 expression; the available phase 2 drug tilpisertib is an inhibitor and is directionally opposite.

- CEDAR2 significant gut eQTL rows for PRKCB: 20.
- CEDAR2 significant PRKCB eQTLs in macrophage-like nodes: 0.
- CEDAR2 macrophage-like nodes: `Node_len_4_pos_6` and `leaf_TC_immune_28_pos_5`.
- CEDAR2 classical and intermediate monocytes show positive PRKCB eQTL lead effects, but not the exact rs7404003 variant.
- The exact rs7404003 effect is significant in CEDAR2 granulocytes, with beta = +0.618 and concordant direction.

The direction is concordant in independent blood myeloid or granulocyte data,
but significant exact-variant replication in colon macrophages was not observed
in the available CEDAR2 significant-eQTL tables.

## MPRA and CRISPRi status

- rs7404003 is already included in the prespecified top-three lentiMPRA package.
- The MPRA design includes both alleles, three 251-bp windows, six donors and resting, LPS, IFN-gamma and IL-23 conditions.
- No MPRA result exists.
- Gene-level PRKCB CRISPRi remains complementary rather than allele-specific.

## Treatment-recommendation boundary

PRKCB is the strongest clinical-stage repurposing hypothesis because its
expression direction and inhibitor action agree. It is not a treatment
recommendation because sotrastaurin is a pan-PKC inhibitor, its UC trial was
small and early-terminated, the efficacy interval crossed zero and confirmatory
target-selective evidence is absent. MAP3K8 should not be advanced with an
inhibitor unless an activation strategy or a direction-reversing mechanism is
established.
