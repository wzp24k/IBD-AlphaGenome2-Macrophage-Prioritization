# Reviewer 2 and Reviewer 3 audit

Date: 2026-11-14

Scope: domain positioning, literature integration, conceptual precision,
practical implications, assumptions and broader validity. Reference numbering
and DOI checks were excluded at the author's request.

## Reviewer 2: domain assessment

### Strengths

- The manuscript integrates IBD genetics, fine-mapping, intestinal macrophage
  biology, immune eQTL data and several perturbation resources.
- The central distinction among credible-set membership, target prediction,
  cell-context selectivity and experimental support is explicit.
- The manuscript distinguishes model predictions, chromatin annotation,
  reporter assays and gene-level perturbation.
- The contribution is an evidence-calibrated prioritization framework, not a
  claim of new disease mechanism.

### Corrections

- Replaced unqualified "macrophage-specific" wording with
  "macrophage-versus-control specificity" where the comparator set was limited
  to T cells and epithelial cells.
- Added the limitation that AlphaGenome was not benchmarked against a matched
  variant-level macrophage gold standard.
- Retained the conclusion that the framework is hypothesis-prioritizing rather
  than a benchmark of overall model accuracy.

### Remaining weakness

The study does not provide a new causal mechanism. This is consistent with the
stated scope but limits the contribution to methodological integration,
prioritization and public-evidence triangulation.

## Reviewer 3: perspective assessment

### Strengths

- The framework is practically useful for deciding which variants should enter
  allele-specific reporter assays or macrophage perturbation experiments.
- Evidence labels and claim-calibration tables make the output auditable for
  multidisciplinary teams.
- The manuscript explicitly reports active-disease limitations, ancestry
  limitations and the absence of new wet-laboratory experiments.

### Corrections

- Added an explicit statement that the score is not a diagnostic, prognostic
  or treatment-selection tool.
- Defined the practical use as experimental triage and target prioritization.
- Stated that clinical translation requires variant-specific perturbation,
  ancestry-matched data and independent disease cohorts.

### Broader limitations

- Public data do not represent all intestinal cell types or donor ancestries.
- A prioritization score could be misused clinically if its evidence boundaries
  were removed from the output.
- Experimental validation and health-equity implications require prospective
  work outside the present study.

## Conclusion

The domain and perspective reviews found no additional internal numerical
contradiction after correction. The remaining limitation is external
experimental and clinical validation, which is already disclosed in the
manuscript.
