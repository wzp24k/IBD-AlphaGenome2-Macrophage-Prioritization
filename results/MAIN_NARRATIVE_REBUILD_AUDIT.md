# Main narrative rebuild

## Revised thesis

The paper is no longer organized as a collection of model scores and
secondary analyses. Its central claim is that non-coding IBD variants require
an evidence ladder.

The ladder separates:

1. Credible-set and target-gene plausibility.
2. Genome-calibrated predicted effects.
3. Macrophage-versus-control specificity.
4. Donor and disease-state stability.
5. Independent genetic and public perturbation evidence.
6. Mechanistic mediation and translational hypotheses.

Prioritization is therefore treated as evidence organization, not causal
proof.

## Candidate archetypes

### PDGFB

PDGFB is the leading macrophage-regulatory archetype:

- Strong fine-mapping and macrophage specificity.
- Exact Crohn DC-macrophage cCRE.
- ABC link to PDGFB.
- Effective gene-level Perturb-seq knockdown.
- No UKB or MVP target-level L2G support.
- No proof that rs2143178 is the causal nucleotide.

### PRKCB

PRKCB is the mechanistic-triangulation archetype:

- Exact-variant mRNA eQTL.
- Public gene-level perturbation evidence.
- Positive IVW and weighted-median MR.
- Significant SMR.
- SuSiE-colocalization H4 = 1.0.
- Nonzero MR-Egger intercept and regional heterogeneity.
- No exact sQTL, pQTL or caQTL coverage.
- Virtual JAK/STAT prediction without experimental causal closure.
- MR leave-one-out direction is positive.
- Local HEIDI windows remain heterogeneous.
- Virtual JAK/STAT remains negative across shrinkage and 1,000 bootstrap replicates.

## Results structure

- Layer 1: credible-set landscape, cell-context controls, Atlas calibration and live specificity.
- Layer 2: donor-derived and disease-state stability.
- Layer 3: independent IBD genetics and benchmarking.
- Layer 4: public perturbation, Perturb-seq and confounding sensitivity.
- Layer 5: LD-aware PRKCB mediation.
- Translation layer: indication-aware hypotheses and treatment boundary.

## Claim boundary

The revised main line supports:

- Evidence stratification for macrophage-focused variant prioritization.
- Complementary candidate archetypes rather than a single universal winner.
- Mechanistic prioritization of PRKCB without allele-specific causal proof.

The revised main line does not support:

- Universal superiority over PIP-only.
- All Top 10 variants being experimentally validated.
- A clinical treatment recommendation.
- Causal closure from MR, colocalization or virtual perturbation alone.
